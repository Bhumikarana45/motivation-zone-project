What is motivation?

Motivation is the inner drive that makes people act and work towards goals. In a workplace, motivated people put in more effort, stay longer and work better together.

  • It gives energy to work
  • It gives direction towards goals
  • It helps people keep going when things get hard
Motivated people build better teams

Types of motivation

Intrinsic

Comes from within
  • Personal satisfaction
  • Learning new skills
  • Achievement
  • Self-improvement

Example: practising a skill because you enjoy getting better at it.

Extrinsic

Comes from outside
  • Salary
  • Bonus
  • Promotion
  • Awards
  • Recognition

Example: working extra hours to earn a bonus.

Chart 2

Maslow's hierarchy of needs

Abraham Maslow (1943) said people are motivated by different needs, starting from basic survival and moving up to personal growth. Once a lower need is met, the next one becomes important.

Chart 3

Herzberg's two-factor theory

Frederick Herzberg (1959) found that the things that stop people being unhappy at work are different from the things that make them truly satisfied.

Hygiene factors

Prevent dissatisfaction
  • Salary
  • Job security
  • Working conditions
  • Company policies

If these are missing, people feel unhappy. Having them alone does not motivate.

Motivators

Create satisfaction
  • Achievement
  • Recognition
  • Responsibility
  • Growth
  • Advancement

These make people enjoy their work and give their best.
